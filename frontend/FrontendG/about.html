ABOUT EDO STATE

A Land of Culture, Heritage & Tradition

Edo State is one of Nigeria's most culturally significant states, located in the southern part of the country. Its capital, Benin City, is widely known for its remarkable history, traditional arts and cultural heritage.

The state is home to the historic Benin Kingdom, one of Africa's renowned ancient kingdoms. Edo's traditions, festivals, architecture, craftsmanship and cuisine continue to tell the story of a people whose heritage has been preserved for generations.

From historical landmarks and museums to beautiful natural environments, Edo offers visitors an opportunity to experience history while enjoying the warmth and hospitality of its people.

OUR HERITAGE

The Rich History of Edo State

A story of kingdoms, creativity, resilience and cultural excellence.

The Benin Kingdom

At the heart of Edo's history is the ancient Benin Kingdom, known for its powerful rulers, sophisticated political system and remarkable artistic traditions.

Benin City

Benin City serves as the cultural heart of Edo State. Its historic sites, museums and traditional institutions provide visitors with a connection to Edo's past.

Benin Art & Craft

Edo is famous for its artistic heritage, particularly bronze casting, traditional sculptures, beadwork and other forms of craftsmanship passed from generation to generation.

Living Culture

Edo's culture continues to thrive through language, festivals, traditional ceremonies, food, music, dance and the everyday lives of its people.
